About us

We connect the shops in your city with the people who deliver for them, and take the awkward parts off both of their hands.

A shop gets its menu in front of the whole city and keeps taking orders the way it already does — including straight from the WhatsApp messages it already receives. A delivery company brings its own fleet and is sent work on merit: the more it completes and the faster it claims, the more it is offered. Nobody has to choose between the two, and nobody has to run their own riders to be on the platform.

The whole thing works in Arabic and English, both ways round, and takes cash as seriously as it takes cards — because most of this city still pays at the door.

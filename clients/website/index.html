Help Center

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about YouDrop? We have collected answers to the most common queries.

How do I sign up as a Merchant?

Simply click the 'Start as Merchant' button, upload your business documents, menu, or store catalog, and our team will audit and activate your account within 24 hours.

What is the YouDrop Butler service?

YouDrop Butler lets you request custom orders from any shop in your city. Select 'Buy Anything' or 'Send Anything' in the app to dispatch a personal courier instantly.

Are delivery fleets managed directly?

Yes. Carriers get access to our unified dispatcher dashboard to onboard custom fleets, plan shifts, and track operations directly through the corporate control tower.

How fast are rider earnings paid out?

Riders are paid instantly. The moment a delivery is confirmed as completed, your earnings and tips are routed directly to your app wallet for immediate cash-out.

Which regions does YouDrop operate in?

We operate across major urban hubs. Check our map widget or search for your city directly in the app to view live coverage and available shops.

What are the platform onboarding fees?

We maintain absolute transparency. There are zero upfront onboarding fees. YouDrop works on a minor transactional commission tier tailored to your volume.
